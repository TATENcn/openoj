Architecture and safety properties
- The sync executor runs in spawn_blocking while the worker remains available for periodic lease
  renewal at the control-plane-negotiated cadence.
- A cancellation response, stale lease or renewal failure invokes an executor-provided,
  least-authority cancellation handle and waits for teardown before discarding the result.
- A final renewal immediately before submit closes the completion-to-submit cancellation race.
- FirecrackerVmCancellation can only start_kill the exact Firecracker child owned by its Attempt;
  it is idempotent and covers cancellation before process registration.
- The guest protocol remains v0alpha1. Existing guest Cancel/Cancelled messages are unchanged and
  are not used for prompt interruption because the current guest agent cannot consume them while
  synchronously waiting for a child workload.
- The judge process survives ordinary cancellation and continues its control loop.

Real-KVM cancellation assertions
- a real Firecracker process reaches an active API socket for an 8-second infinite-loop request
- control-plane cancellation persists state=cancelled and terminal_result=true
- the Attempt-owned Firecracker child is interrupted rather than waiting for the 8-second stage
  deadline
- Firecracker API and vsock paths disappear in less than 3 seconds
- the judge process remains alive after ordinary cancellation
- CLI status remains responsive and the cancelled terminal cannot be overwritten

Unit and integration coverage
- Firecracker cancellation kills only its owned child and repeated cancellation is safe
- blocking execution is interrupted on a control-plane Cancel response
- blocking execution is interrupted when lease renewal reports a stale lease
- a final pre-submit renewal fences cancellation that lands after execution completion
- cancellation before Firecracker child registration is retained and applied after spawn
